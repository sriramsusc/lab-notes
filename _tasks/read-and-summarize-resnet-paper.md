## What needs to be done

Read He et al. 2016 and write the summary up on its paper page. The part we
actually need is the degradation argument in Section 3.1 — why deeper plain
networks train *worse*, and why that is an optimization problem rather than an
overfitting one.

## Definition of done

- Summary submitted through the form and showing on the paper page
- The degradation experiment is explained clearly enough to cite in the draft
- Any follow-up papers worth reading are added to the queue
